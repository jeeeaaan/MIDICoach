Files in this folder: 30 mp3 recordings (A0, C8 and A/C/Ds/Fs of octaves 1-7), copied
unmodified from the mp3 versions published by the Tone.js project:
https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
(README-original.txt is the author's original README, which states "Licence: CC-by".)

Changes made to the original work (CC BY 3.0, section 3(b)): the recordings were
converted from the original 48 kHz / 24-bit multi-velocity-layer set to a single
velocity layer in 128 kbps mp3 (by the Tone.js project, not by this application). At
playback, this application changes the playback rate (at most +/-1.5 semitones) to
obtain notes between the recorded ones, and applies its own volume envelope.

The original author does not endorse this application.
